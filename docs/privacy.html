Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. What data we access

When you authorize kozyrbek autopost ("the App") with TikTok, the App receives an access token and a refresh token, and basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) through the user.info.basic scope. The App uses the video.publish and video.upload scopes only to upload videos that you have prepared.

2. How we use data

Data is used only to publish your videos to your own TikTok account at the times you choose. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored in encrypted secret storage and are used only by the publishing process. Videos and captions are stored only for as long as needed to publish them.

4. Your rights

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). After revocation, tokens stop working and are deleted. You can request deletion of any stored data by contacting us.

5. Children

The App is not intended for users under 13 years of age.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.

7. Contact

Questions or data requests: oumountoumov@gmail.com
